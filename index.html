Her story

Charissa paints because the Pacific Northwest never stops giving her something to look at: fog lifting off a ridge, a fern uncurling, light on a river stone.

What began as painting for the joy of it grew into a studio full of canvases, stones and wood rounds, each finished with texture you can see and feel. This is where she shares that work, and where you can find a piece that feels like home.

Meet Charissa

Inspiration & process

The studio starts outdoors. Charissa gathers ideas on trails and shorelines, then builds each piece in layers.

Forests

Evergreens, ferns and mossy trunks.

Water & mist

Rivers, coastline and mountain fog.

Seasons

Wildflowers, autumn leaves and mushrooms.

Texture

Brushwork, stone and wood grain, felt in every piece.

Room for sketches, works in progress and studio photos.
